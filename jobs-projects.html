Our listing standard

Every listing tells you the truth before you apply.

  1. 01

    Verified employer

    Every listing shows whether the organisation and its representative have completed AdorWorks' employer verification.

  2. 02

    Clear terms up front

    Pay, place, closing date, required skills and the selection process — before you apply.

  3. 03

    No off-platform bait

    No phone numbers or contact details that pull you away from AdorWorks' protection.

  4. 04

    Fair selection

    Shortlists by skill, checked by a person — not who applied first or who someone already knows. You never pay to apply.
