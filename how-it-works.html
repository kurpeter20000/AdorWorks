How it works

From brief to completed work.

A person checks every engagement at each step, from the first brief to the final payment.

  1. 1

    Submit a brief

    The role, project or service, with budget, timing and any location or safety needs.

  2. 2

    Get verified

    We confirm your organisation, your representative and who can decide, before sharing anyone.

  3. 3

    Review a shortlist

    A matcher recommends three to five candidates, or a managed team.

  4. 4

    Select and contract

    Interview support, a confirmed scope, and a project, contract or placement agreement.

  5. 5

    Track delivery

    Milestones, risks, approvals and changes, with a named account contact.

  6. 6

    Complete and review

    Acceptance is recorded, invoices issued, and both sides leave a review.

  1. 1

    Create your profile

    Sign up, confirm your phone or email, and agree to the code of conduct.

  2. 2

    Submit your evidence

    Identity, portfolio and references. This earns your Registered badge.

  3. 3

    Complete an assessment

    A practical test made for your category of work.

  4. 4

    Get matched

    Considered for briefs that fit your skills, time and rate, reviewed by a person.

  5. 5

    Deliver with support

    A clear scope, milestone tracking and an AdorWorks contact.

  6. 6

    Get paid and grow

    Documented payout after the client approves, plus feedback that builds your profile.

If something goes wrong

A calm, written process. Nobody is left guessing.

Read the Trust & Safety policy →
  1. 01

    The affected milestone is paused and both sides are told.

  2. 02

    We review the brief, contract, messages, files and timeline.

  3. 03

    We help agree a fix: a correction, a new milestone, partial acceptance or a replacement.

  4. 04

    Fraud, safety, harassment or legal concerns go to leadership and advisers.

  5. 05

    The outcome is recorded, private details protected, and our policy improved.
