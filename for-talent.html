Fair expectation

Creating a profile does not guarantee work. AdorWorks assesses eligibility and fit for each opportunity. We do not sell access to legitimate employment, and we will never ask you to pay a recruitment fee to secure a job.

Who this is for

Emerging talent

High potential but limited work history. We support you with assessment, supervised projects and portfolio evidence.

Market-ready freelancer

You can work independently. We bring qualified demand, safer contracts and stronger visibility.

Experienced professional

Seeking contract or full-time work. We offer confidential matching and credible employers.

Service business or team

You can deliver a defined outcome. We provide a storefront, leads and transaction support.

Diaspora or regional specialist

You can contribute remotely or on assignment. We give you a structured way to engage.

What each verification tier means

Badges say what was actually checked — not a vague trust signal.

Registered

Phone/email confirmed, a minimum profile completed, and agreement to AdorWorks' code of conduct. A real account — capability not yet verified.

Identity verified

Government-issued identification checked and securely recorded. Identity has been reviewed.

AdorVerified

Identity, portfolio, references, an interview and a category-specific assessment. Evidence supports ability to perform the listed work.

AdorCertified

A live simulation, client-readiness assessment and a successful platform engagement. Demonstrated work quality and reliability.

Team lead

Multiple successful engagements, communication strength and a leadership review. Eligible to lead managed squads.

We use realistic work samples rather than certificate-only screening, and every assessment includes a clear pass, development or re-assessment path. An automated score is never presented as a complete judgment of your worth or employability.

Getting client-ready

Before you apply, a strong profile makes the biggest difference.

  • A clear professional headline and summary — what you do and who you do it for.
  • Real work samples, not just a list of skills — the portfolio is what gets you shortlisted.
  • Honest availability and rate expectations, so matches are workable from the start.
  • Readiness basics: a working device, stable-enough connectivity and, where relevant, power backup.
  • References who will actually confirm what you've told us.
