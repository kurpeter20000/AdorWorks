Simple principle: employers pay for reduced risk. Talent never pays to work.

Today, AdorWorks charges a 0% platform fee while we're in our founding pilot — nothing is deducted from any payment. The model below is what we're validating for later, not a live price list.

Fair recruitment

Basic talent registration and access to legitimate employment opportunities is free, and always will be. This follows ILO guidance that workers and jobseekers should not be charged recruitment fees or related costs.

Right now

AdorWorks is not currently charging a platform fee. Employers pay talent and contractors the full agreed amount directly, with no percentage held back. The table below describes the pricing model we're testing for when fees go live — none of it is in effect today, and we'll announce clearly before that changes.

None of the rates above are active — AdorWorks charges 0% today. If and when a fee is switched on for a category, it will be confirmed with you before any engagement is booked, and nothing will ever be charged without your agreement.

Enterprise and framework pricing

NGOs, larger companies and agencies running recurring needs — talent squads, framework agreements, contractor-of-record support — usually make more sense on a custom arrangement than a per-project fee.
