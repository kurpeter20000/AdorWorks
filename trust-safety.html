A marketplace built on proof.

We verify talent and employers, define every scope, track milestones and stay accountable when something goes wrong.

Verification framework

Five tiers. Each one is earned, and each one can be checked.

Tier 1 of 5

Registered

An account with the basics in place. Skills are not yet checked.

What's checked

  • Phone or email confirmed
  • Minimum profile completed
  • Code of conduct accepted

Tier 2 of 5

Identity verified

We have checked who you are.

What's checked

  • Government ID checked
  • ID securely recorded

Tier 3 of 5

AdorVerified

Your skills are shown with real evidence.

What's checked

  • Identity verified
  • Portfolio and references reviewed
  • Interview
  • Category assessment passed

Tier 4 of 5

AdorCertified

Fully verified, with your documents on record and your skills proven through real, paid work. You're eligible to work and ready for clients.

What's checked

  • Documents verified and recorded
  • Live work simulation
  • Client-readiness assessment
  • A successful engagement on AdorWorks

Tier 5 of 5

Team lead

Trusted to lead managed squads.

What's checked

  • Several successful engagements
  • Strong communication
  • Leadership review

How we verify employers

  1. Organisation registration or proof of operations
  2. A verified representative with decision-making authority
  3. Corporate email or domain, plus phone check
  4. Role, scope and budget defined before matching
  5. Agreement to fair-hiring and payment terms
  6. Deposit or approved credit for higher-risk work
  7. Extra review for overseas, relocation, field-safety or vulnerable-group work

How money is handled

We never call client funds "escrow" or hold them informally. Pre-funding runs through a licensed, documented process, with an audit trail from scope to payout.

Legal and ethical foundation

South Sudan's Labour Act includes licensing for private employment agencies. We are confirming every requirement before we scale full-time or cross-border placement.

  • Agency licensing
  • Worker classification
  • Contracts and safety
  • Tax and cross-border pay
  • Data privacy
  • Anti-discrimination and grievances
