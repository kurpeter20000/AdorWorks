Why you don't see a public directory

We don't publish talent contact details or full profiles to anonymous visitors — private phone numbers, email addresses and identity documents stay behind AdorWorks' review until a shortlist is formally presented to a verified employer. This protects professionals from scraping and off-platform circumvention, and it's how we keep every introduction accountable. Read more on Trust & Safety.
