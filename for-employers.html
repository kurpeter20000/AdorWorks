Employer verification

We verify you before we share anyone with you.

  1. Organisation registration or other proof you operate
  2. A verified representative who can make decisions
  3. Corporate email or domain where available, plus phone check
  4. A clear role, scope, budget, workplace and reporting line
  5. Agreement to fair hiring, non-discrimination, confidentiality and payment terms
  6. A deposit or approved credit terms before higher-risk work
  7. Extra review for overseas placements, relocation, field safety or vulnerable groups

Payments and protection

We don't hold your money in informal accounts or call it "escrow". Pre-funding runs through a licensed, documented process:

  1. Signed scope
  2. Deposit
  3. Milestone evidence
  4. Your approval
  5. Invoice
  6. Payout to talent

Our licensed local payment partner is being finalised. Until then, work is scoped and invoiced directly.

Questions employers ask

How fast can I get a shortlist?

Usually within 10 business days of confirming your brief, depending on how specialised the role is.

What if my need doesn't fit the three categories?

Send it anyway. The categories show our current strengths, not our limits.

What if the person or team doesn't work out?

Every engagement has a named account contact and a written replacement process.

Do you only work with South Sudanese clients?

No. Diaspora and East African clients are welcome.

What does it cost?

Fees depend on how you hire; see Pricing. Talent never pays to be considered. Employers pay for matching, support and accountability.
