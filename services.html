Commission a defined outcome, not a stranger.

Each service below is a packaged deliverable, matched to a quality-controlled provider and confirmed by an AdorWorks team member before work starts — not an open bid to the lowest price.

On pricing

We don't publish fixed prices until provider costs, revision scope and category demand are properly tested — quoting an unsupported number would be a promise we can't reliably keep. Request a service below and we'll confirm scope and price together before anything is booked. See the fee model on Pricing.

Creative & media

Brand & graphic design

Inputs needed: brand brief, references, existing assets.
Typical timeframe: confirmed per scope.
Excludes: full brand strategy (see AdorWorks' sister company, Adormedia).

Photography & video production

Deliverable: a shoot day plus edited assets to an agreed spec.
Inputs needed: shot list, location access, usage rights terms.
Typical timeframe: confirmed per scope.
Excludes: equipment insurance and location permits (client's responsibility unless separately agreed).

Editing & animation

Deliverable: edited video, motion graphics or animated explainer to spec.
Inputs needed: raw footage or brief, reference examples.
Typical timeframe: confirmed per scope.
Excludes: original filming (pair with photography & video production).

Content creation & copywriting

Deliverable: written or social content to an agreed brief and word count.
Inputs needed: tone-of-voice guidance, key messages, examples.
Typical timeframe: confirmed per scope.
Excludes: translation into additional languages (request separately).
