Proof over promises.

We will not measure success by registrations, promises or publicity. We will measure it by trusted employers, completed work, fair payments, stronger careers — and the number of people whose first credible professional opportunity came through AdorWorks.

How a story earns its place here

Every case study we publish follows the same method.

  1. Written consent from both the client and the professional — with anonymised publication offered where needed.
  2. A recorded starting problem and baseline: time, cost, quality, reach, capacity or vacancy duration.
  3. An honest account of why the person or team was selected, and what AdorWorks actually verified.
  4. The real work, timeframe, milestones and support AdorWorks provided.
  5. Reported evidence — outputs, completion time, satisfaction, efficiency change or talent earnings.
  6. A quote included only after it's been approved by the person who said it.
  7. Limitations stated plainly — we never invent a result or expose confidential client information.
